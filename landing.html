When should my child begin preparing with Learnly? expand_more

Most families begin in Year 4 (consolidating core mathematical agility and classical vocabulary roots) or early Year 5 (advancing to all 21 GL question typologies and full timed mock simulations).

How does the Standard Age Score (SAS) calibration work? expand_more

Official 11+ providers (GL Assessment and CEM) calibrate test scores using an actuarial normal distribution with mean 100 and standard deviation 15. A summer-born child (July/August) receives statistical adjustment points to ensure fair parity with September-born peers.

Yes. You can toggle between GL Assessment (all 21 standard question types), CEM Select (speed cloze passages and numerical agility), and CSSE Essex papers directly in the Practice Arena.
