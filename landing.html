Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Learnly 11+.

When should my child start preparing with Learnly? expand_more

Most families begin in Year 4 (building strong vocabulary and foundational mathematical agility) or early Year 5 (advancing to all 21 GL question typologies and full timed mock exams). Learnly adapts to your child regardless of whether they have 12 months or 8 weeks until their test.

How does the Standard Age Score (SAS) calculation work? expand_more

The 11+ does not rely on simple raw percentages. Official exam bodies (GL Assessment and CEM) calibrate scores using an actuarial normal distribution with a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15. A summer-born child (born in July or August) is compared against other children of the exact same chronological age, typically receiving up to 6 statistical adjustment points to ensure fair parity with September-born peers.

Does Learnly cover both GL Assessment and CEM Select formats? expand_more

Yes. You can toggle between GL Assessment (all 21 standard question types), CEM Select (speed-based cloze passages, shuffled sentences, numerical fluency), and CSSE Essex papers directly in the Practice Arena.

Can I cancel at any time? expand_more

Yes, completely. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees. You can manage and cancel your subscription with a single click inside the Parent Oversight Portal at any time.
