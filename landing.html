[ I. THE ARGUMENT ]

Why raw percentages fail the candidate.

Every September, thousands of pupils score above 85% on commercial paper workbooks, yet receive rejection letters from Queen Elizabeth’s, Henrietta Barnett, and the Sutton grammar schools.

Grammar school consortia do not admit pupils on raw marks. They rank candidates on Standard Age Scores (SAS) calculated through Gaussian distribution curves. A child born in August receives up to six raw points of age-standardisation credit over a peer born in September of the previous year. Static workbooks cannot compute this; Learnly executes it on every question.
